Arcade survival

Survive the dying star.

Ten minutes. One thumb. One way out.

Starbreach drops you into the corona of a collapsing sun. Steer with one thumb while your ship fires on its own, collect solar shards to level up, and pick one of three upgrades each time. Pair a weapon with the right module and it evolves. A Warden arrives every three minutes. Last ten, and the Void Heart comes for you. Break it, and you escape.

Between runs, the cores you bring home buy permanent upgrades and new starting weapons in the Hangar.
