Support

Need a hand, pilot?

Email brax6@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. You'll get a reply within two business days.

Purchases

A purchase didn't arrive. Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. No Ads and the Pilot Pack come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Core crates are delivered once per purchase, and restoring them won't add them twice.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Ads only appear between runs, never during one. Video ads you choose to watch (revive, reroll, double cores) are always optional. No Ads removes the between-run ads and makes those rewards instant.

If you're in a region that requires it, you can change your ad privacy choices any time under Settings › Ad Privacy Choices in the game (the gear on the home screen).

Progress

Your cores, upgrades and records are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Purchases you own can be restored, but unspent cores can't be recovered.

How to play

Drag anywhere to fly; your ship fires on its own. Pick up solar shards to level up and choose an upgrade. Survive ten minutes and destroy the Void Heart to escape.
