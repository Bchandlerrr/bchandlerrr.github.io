Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective October 26, 2026. This policy covers the Starbreach app for iPhone and iPad, made by Braxton Chandler ("we").

In short: we don't run accounts, servers or analytics. Your game progress stays on your device. The only third party that receives data is Google, which serves the ads, and only after any consent your region requires.

Data we keep

Game progress (cores, upgrades, records and settings) is stored only on your device. We never receive it.

Advertising

Starbreach shows ads through Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, the app runs Google's User Messaging Platform, which asks for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states). Google may collect device and usage data such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device model and settings, an advertising or vendor identifier, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Starbreach does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier for tracking.

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can change your choices any time under Settings › Ad Privacy Choices in the game (the gear on the home screen) (where available), or remove between-run ads by buying No Ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation of what you bought.

Children

Starbreach is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete; removing the app deletes your saved progress. For data Google holds, use Google's tools linked above. For any question, email brax6@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
