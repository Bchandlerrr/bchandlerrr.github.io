Through the Gap · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: Through the Gap has no accounts, and the developer doesn't collect your name, email, contacts or location. Your progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers and approximate location (from your IP address) to serve and measure ads. Remove Ads (or the Neon Bundle) stops the banner and the ads between levels.

What the game stores

  • Game progress (stars, coins, skins, daily streak) is saved in a file on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and the developer cannot see it.
  • Settings (sound, haptics) are stored on your device only.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game submits your total stars and best daily streak to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where they appear with your Game Center name. Apple handles this under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad on the level map (never during play), an occasional full-screen ad between levels (not before level 6, at most one every three levels and every three minutes), and optional reward videos you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, cap and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, crash and performance diagnostics, and your ad interactions. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy Options. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.

The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The game never sees your payment details, only which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

Through the Gap is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app deletes your progress. For advertising data, use Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
