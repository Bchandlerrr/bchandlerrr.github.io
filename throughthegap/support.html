Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: email . Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number if it's about a level.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to fire one laser pulse from the emitter on the left. It flies straight across. Gates between you and the target have gaps that slide up and down. If the pulse touches a gate, the shot is wasted. Time the tap so it passes every gap and hits the target.

How do stars work?

Win with your first shot for 3 stars, your second for 2, and any later shot for 1. Tap retry (top right) any time to start the level again with full shots.

Is every level really beatable?

Yes. Every level is generated from a fixed seed and checked by a solver that finds the winning moments, so each one has a fair timing window that comes back regularly. Watch the rhythm: gaps line up on a repeating beat.

I ran out of shots

Retry is free and instant. If you like, an optional short video gives you 3 more shots or lets you skip the level. After a win, another optional video doubles your coins. You never have to watch one.

Restoring purchases

Open Settings (the gear on the map) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Remove Ads and the Neon Bundle (with its Aurora laser) come back automatically. Coin packs are consumable and are added once, when bought.

Ads and privacy choices

Remove Ads (or the Neon Bundle) stops the map banner and the ads between levels. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK), you can change your ad consent any time in Settings › Privacy Options. See the Privacy Policy.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
