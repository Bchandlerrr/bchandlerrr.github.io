SpaceMiner

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version. SpaceMiner has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or location. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect limited device and usage information to show and measure them. SpaceMiner never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your save: minerals, credits, Dark Matter, Cosmic Shards, upgrade and perk levels, daily-reward streak, achievements and the mining history behind the Mining Report. It is written to a file in the app's own storage and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your settings, such as whether haptics are on.

The developer runs no servers for SpaceMiner and receives none of this data.

Ads from Google AdMob

The free game can show a short full-screen ad every few screen changes, and you can choose to watch a video for a reward (Overdrive, or doubling an offline haul). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure them, Google may collect your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and operating-system details, information about the app, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Because SpaceMiner never requests tracking permission, your device's advertising identifier is not shared with Google.

Where the law requires consent, such as in the EEA, the UK and some US states, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Sponsor Consent. Google's practices are described in its privacy policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Dark Matter crates and the Infinite Fuel Pass are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; SpaceMiner only learns which items you own so it can unlock them. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

SpaceMiner is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Progress to erase your save, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. To manage Google's ad data, visit My Ad Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
