Common questions

How do I earn credits?

Tap the asteroid (or let your drones work) to collect minerals, then press Sell All Minerals. Credits pay for upgrades on the Upgrades tab.

Does the rig keep mining when the app is closed?

Yes, for up to 8 hours by default. When you return, a report shows what came in while you were away, and you can watch a short video to double it. The Automated Haulers perk removes the 8-hour limit.

What is a Supernova?

Once your run has earned enough, you can trigger a Supernova from the Mining tab. Minerals, credits and upgrade levels reset, and you receive Cosmic Shards to spend on permanent perks in Black Market → Shard Perks. Dark Matter, Shard Perks and the Infinite Fuel Pass carry over.

What does the Infinite Fuel Pass do?

It permanently doubles all mining and turns off the full-screen ads between screens. Optional reward videos stay available, since watching them is always your choice.

I bought the Fuel Pass on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings → Restore Fuel Pass while signed in with the same Apple Account. Dark Matter crates are consumed when bought, so they can't be restored.

Is my progress backed up?

Your save lives on this device only. Deleting the app deletes the save, so keep the app installed if you're moving data with a device backup.

How do I turn off vibration?

Settings → Haptics.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset Progress. This erases everything, including Cosmic Shards, and can't be undone. A purchased Fuel Pass stays yours.
