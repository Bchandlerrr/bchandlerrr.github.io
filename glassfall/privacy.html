Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective October 27, 2026. Covers the Glassfall app for iPhone and iPad by Braxton Chandler ("we").

In short: no accounts, no servers, no analytics of our own. Your progress stays on your device. Google, which serves the ads, is the only third party that receives data, and only after any consent your region requires.

Data on your device

Shards, upgrades, settings and any vigil in progress are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Advertising

Glassfall shows ads through Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested the app runs Google's User Messaging Platform, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Google may collect your IP address (for approximate location), device information, an advertising or vendor identifier and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Glassfall does not ask to track you across other companies' apps. See how Google uses this data. You can change your choices in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices (where offered) or buy No Ads.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. We never see your payment details.

Children

Glassfall is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.
