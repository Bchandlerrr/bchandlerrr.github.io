A roguelite in stained glass

Shatter the dark.

The Gloam has crept into the cathedral. You are its last Glazier. Aim from the top of the rose window and let a glass orb fall: every pane it strikes turns to light against the shadows creeping down the nave. Strike gold and the whole shot turns critical. Crack a leaded pane twice and it bursts.

Climb three acts of rooms, choose new orbs, find relics, temper your glass at shrines and face the keeper of each floor. Fall, and the shards you gathered fire new upgrades in the Kiln.
