How it works

Tap a tile to sneak one step, or Wait to stay put. Guards walk their dotted routes back and forth; their flashlight covers the two tiles ahead. Dashed tiles light up next turn. Grab at least one gem to open the exit; all three in par moves is a perfect heist.

Common questions

  • Is every vault solvable? Yes. Every daily and practice vault is checked by a solver before you see it, and par is the true minimum.
  • When does the daily change? At midnight on your phone. Everyone gets the same vault that day.
  • What are keys for? A hint (your best next step) or a rewind after you're busted. You start with 3, earn one each day you escape the daily, and can watch an optional video for another.
