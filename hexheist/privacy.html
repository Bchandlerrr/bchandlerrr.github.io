Hex Heist · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: Hex Heist has no accounts, and the developer doesn't collect your name, email, contacts or location. Your progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers and approximate location (from your IP address) to serve and measure ads. Remove Ads (or the Master Key Bundle) stops the banner and the ads between vaults.

What the game stores

  • Game progress (daily results, streak, practice vaults cleared, keys, archive scores) is saved on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and the developer cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner on the home screen (never during a heist), can show a full-screen ad between practice vaults (at most one every three vaults and every three minutes, and never during the daily heist), and offers optional reward videos you choose to watch for a free key or to rewind a move. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, cap and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, crash and performance diagnostics, and your ad interactions. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in Shop › Ad privacy. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.

The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Live game settings

When you open the game, it downloads a small settings file from this website (things like how often ads appear, key rewards and an occasional announcement). The request carries no personal information from the game. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address to deliver the file.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The game never sees your payment details, only which items you own so it can unlock them. Keys are an in-game item with no cash value.

Children

Hex Heist is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app deletes your progress. For advertising data, use Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
