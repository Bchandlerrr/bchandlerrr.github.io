Idle game for iPhone & iPad

Gridlock: Idle Traffic Tycoon

Most traffic games ask you to fix the jam. Gridlock asks you to cause it. Honk, hire the worst drivers in the city, and watch the street fill up until every brake light is glowing.

Download on the App Store

Honk to earn

Every tap honks. Fill the Road Rage meter and every honk is worth five.

Cause the jam

Confused tourists, double-parked trucks, pothole crews, parades and the Presidential Motorcade. Everything you hire shows up on the street.

End Rush Hour

Clear the streets to earn Notoriety, then spend it on perks that make every future jam bigger.

Something new every day

Daily challenges, a daily toll streak, the gold Lucky Cab, and new rides to earn.
