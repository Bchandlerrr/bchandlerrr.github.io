Stuck in traffic?

Help for Gridlock, the idle game where you cause the jam.

Email brax6@icloud.com with your iOS version and what happened. Replies usually take 1–2 days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Garage → Store → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Will I lose progress on a new phone?

No. Progress syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, and your jam will be waiting.

Why did my honks slow down?

Your jam has peaked. Once you're well past the point where you can end Rush Hour, earnings slow. End Rush Hour on the Rush Hour tab to earn Notoriety, which boosts every future jam.

What does VIP include?

VIP removes the banner and the ads between Rush Hours, and gives you every ad reward (Clear a Lane, ×2 boost, double offline) instantly, without watching a video.

Where do I change ad privacy choices?

If your region requires it, Garage → Privacy Options appears. See the Privacy Policy for details.
