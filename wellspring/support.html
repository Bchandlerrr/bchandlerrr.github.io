River running dry?

Help for River Tycoon, the idle game where you grow a river from a spring to the sea.

Email brax6@icloud.com with your iOS version and what happened. Replies usually take 1–2 days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Store → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Will I lose progress on a new phone?

No. Progress syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, and your river will be waiting.

Why did my Flow slow down?

Your river has peaked. Once you're well past the point where it can reach the sea, new Flow comes in more slowly (rewards still pay in full). Open the Sea tab and let it reach the sea to earn Silt, which boosts every river after.

What does No Ads include?

No Ads removes the banner and the ads between trips to the sea, and gives you every ad reward (Grow free, double offline Flow) instantly, without watching a video.

Where do I change ad privacy choices?

If your region requires it, Store → Ad privacy choices appears. See the Privacy Policy for details.
