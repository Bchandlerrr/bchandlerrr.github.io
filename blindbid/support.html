How scoring works

Your score is the hidden value of every lot you won, plus the cash you didn't spend. Three lots of one category are worth 1.5x together; four or more are worth 2x. Ties between bids above $0 are settled by a fair coin shown on screen. If both sides pass, the lot goes unsold.

Common questions

  • Is this gambling? No. All money in Blind Bid is pretend. It can't be bought, won, withdrawn or exchanged for anything, and nothing sold in the app is random.
  • Do purchases help me win? No. Purchases are cosmetic (table themes, paddles) or remove ads. They never change lot values, odds, hints or rivals.
  • Can rivals see my bid? No. A rival decides from the same public information you see: category, size, the seller's note, both cash piles and earlier revealed bids.
  • Why can I only play the Daily Draft once? It's ranked, so everyone gets one attempt at the same lots. Starting it uses the attempt. A new draft opens at midnight UTC.
  • Restore purchases: Settings (gear icon) › Restore Purchases, or Shop › Store › Restore Purchases.
  • Ad privacy choices: where they apply (for example in the EEA and UK), Settings › Ad Privacy Choices.
