Sealed-bid auction duel

Read the lot.
Seal your bid.
Beat the rival.

You and a rival each get $20 of pretend money and ten sealed lots. You see the category, a rough size and a seller's note that might be a bluff. Bid in secret; the higher bid takes the lot. When the gavel falls on lot ten, every hidden value is revealed.

What's inside

Four learnable rivals
Penny lowballs, Duke burns cash early, Hawk waits for big lots, Echo copies your last bid. None of them can see your bid or the hidden values.
Daily Draft
The same ten lots for every player each day, one ranked try, and a Game Center leaderboard.
Campaign
Six rivals, each sharper than the last, ending with The Curator.
Collect sets
Three of a category pays 1.5x, four or more pays double.
Cosmetics only
Coins from matches unlock table themes, paddles and rival hats. Purchases never change values or odds.
Pretend money
No cash prizes, no cash-out, nothing random for sale. It's a game of nerve and skill.
