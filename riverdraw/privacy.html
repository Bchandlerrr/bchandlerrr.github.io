River Draw · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

The short version: River Draw has no accounts, and the developer doesn't collect your name, email or location. Your progress is saved on your device and in your own iCloud. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers and approximate location (from your IP address) to serve and measure ads. You can buy No Ads to stop the ads between rivers.

Information River Draw stores

  • Game progress (stars, coins, hints, daily streak, cosmetics, River Pass progress) is stored on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. The developer cannot access it.
  • Settings such as sound and haptics are stored on your device only.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, River Draw submits your daily river score, total stars and endless rivers to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where they appear with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of River Draw shows occasional ads between rivers (never before your 10th finished river, and at most one every three minutes) and optional rewarded video ads. These are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit the frequency of and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and app information, crash and performance diagnostics, and your ad interactions. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), River Draw asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.

River Draw does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Notifications

If you allow them, River Draw schedules local reminders on your device (a new daily river, your streak). Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. River Draw never sees your payment information. It only receives confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them.

Children

River Draw is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the local save. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for River Draw in iOS Settings, or delete it under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. For advertising data, see Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
