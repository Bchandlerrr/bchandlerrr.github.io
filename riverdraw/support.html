Stuck on a valley?

Help for River Draw, the puzzle game where you draw a river from a spring to the sea.

Email brax6@icloud.com with your iOS version and what happened. Replies usually take 1–2 days.

Common questions

How do stars work?

The percent shows how much water reaches the goal, compared with the designer's best river. 60% earns one star, 80% two, and 100% three. Find a better route than the designer's and you'll see "Better than perfect!"

My water stopped. Why?

Water only flows downhill on levels with contour lines (follow the arrows). A beaver dam holds back any river weaker than the number on it, so merge more springs first, or go around. Sand soaks up water as it passes.

How do I split my river?

Start a new line from any point on an existing river. The water divides equally between the branches.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Shop → Restore purchases (or Settings). Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Will I lose progress on a new phone?

No. Progress syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on.

What does No Ads include?

No Ads removes the ads between rivers for good. Optional reward videos (for a hint, extra ink or double coins) stay available but are never required.

When does the daily river change?

At midnight UTC, at the same moment for everyone, so the daily leaderboard is fair.
