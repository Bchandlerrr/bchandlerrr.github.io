Degen Tycoon · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version: Degen Tycoon collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game is saved only on your device.

What the game stores

  • Game progress (virtual cash, coin holdings, upgrades, the market chart and the event feed) is saved on your device using iOS's standard app storage. It never leaves your device and the developer cannot access it.

Purchases

The optional Insider Dev Wallet is processed entirely by Apple. Degen Tycoon never sees your payment information; it only receives confirmation from Apple that you own the item so it can unlock it. All money in the game is virtual, has no real-world value and cannot be withdrawn.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

Degen Tycoon contains no third-party SDKs. It does not show ads, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Degen Tycoon is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your saved game from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
