Degen Tycoon · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version: Degen Tycoon has no accounts, and the developer doesn’t collect your name, email or location. Your game is saved only on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers and approximate location (from your IP address) to serve and measure ads. Buying No Ads (or the Insider Dev Wallet) removes banner and pop-up ads.

What the game stores

  • Game progress (virtual cash, coin holdings, upgrades, streaks, the market chart and the event feed) is saved on your device using iOS’s standard app storage. It never leaves your device and the developer cannot access it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a small banner, occasional full-screen ads after you sell (at most one every three minutes, and never in your first sessions), and optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch for in-game bonuses. These are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit the frequency of and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and app information, crash and performance diagnostics, and your ad interactions. This is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), Degen Tycoon asks for your consent using Google’s consent tool before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Shop → Ad privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.

Degen Tycoon does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Notifications

If you allow them, Degen Tycoon schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your bots have farmed cash or your daily bag is ready). Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Degen Tycoon never sees your payment information; it only receives confirmation of which items you own so it can unlock them. All money in the game is virtual, has no real-world value and cannot be withdrawn.

Children

Degen Tycoon is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your saved game from your device. For advertising data, see Google’s controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
